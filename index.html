What we do

We connect brands with Africa’s fans.

African Connexion is a sports and entertainment partnership agency. We match international brands with the right properties and talent across Africa, and run the partnership on the ground.

Africa’s fans are among the most passionate in the world. The market around them is fragmented, and the best deals are still open. We bring the pieces together for international brands.

On the ground in Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal, The Gambia, Sierra Leone, Kenya and Uganda. Connected to rights holders and talent across the rest of the continent.
